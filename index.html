Need a way to engage your students?

Raise a Pancho could be the way to go. The website is designed with your students in mind.

  1. It offers a new and fun way to engage your students.

  2. It provides with a clear and fun way to hand-in homework, making it into a way of taking care of your virtual pet.

  3. Your students' Pancho is customizable through in-game currency, so they will have an active goal to work towards.

  4. It provides an easy way to check how your students are doing. Their virtual pet will show you how much they have been taking care of.
